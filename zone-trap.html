Breaking the Zone Trap

Timnath sets up in a zone and traps the first pass in the corner. Here's how the trap works, where it leaves people open, and how we beat it. Press Play or step through each diagram.

Catch it high. The corner catches at the free throw line extended, facing up the floor, not deep in the corner where the trap wants you.
Out in 2 seconds. Ball high, pivot away from the trap, no dribbling into it. Pass over the top to the middle or back to the big.
Then attack. Beating the trap usually means 3-on-2 or 4-on-3 the other way. Push it and finish with a layup.

The trap and how we break it

How to teach it

Teach it in this order before Timnath on the 16th. Run it against the JV and add extra defenders, so 5 defenders feels easy on game day. Before you start, teach the JV the trap with one rule: when the ball is caught in a corner, the two closest trap and everyone else takes away the middle and the sideline. If the JV don't run it like Timnath does, the reps don't help.

  • Catch high
  • Ball up, pivot away
  • Two seconds
  • Big steps in
  • Flash behind the trap
  • Score it

  1. Walk-through · 5 min · 5 on 0, then 5 on 5 at half speed

    Walk through the spacing and both breaks from the diagrams. Make the JV trap at half speed so everyone sees where the gaps open.

    • The big inbounds every time, then steps in under the basket right after the pass.
    • The corners pop out to the free throw line extended. Don't drift deep into the corner.
    • The middle guard waits at half court until the trap comes, then flashes.

  2. Trap escape · 8 min · 3 on 2

    A corner catches with two JV trapping, and the big and the middle guard are the outlets. Rotate so everyone gets trapped.

    • Ball high, elbows out, pivot away. Never pick up the dribble going into the trap.
    • The trap gets 2 seconds. Count it out loud. If it lasts longer, it's a turnover.
    • Pass by the trappers' ears, not between them.

  3. Middle flash · 8 min · 4 on 4

    The big, a corner, the middle guard and the far horn, against two trappers, a middle defender and a safety. Read it live: if the middle is open, hit it. If the middle defender jumps it, the ball goes back to the big.

    • The flash comes from half court into the gap and catches at the 3-point line, facing the ball.
    • On the catch, turn and look up the floor right away. The horns should already be running.

  4. Overload · 12 min · 5 on 7

    Our five against the JV's five plus two extra. Use the extras to jump the reversal to the big and sit in the middle. It forces faster decisions than any team will.

    • 10-second count to half court.
    • Reps after a make, too. Grab it, step out, and inbound before the press sets.

  5. Break and score · 10 min · 5 on 5 full court

    The break doesn't end at half court. Play it live to a shot.

    • Offense: 1 point for crossing half court, 2 for a layup.
    • Defense: 2 points for a steal or a 10-second violation.

  6. Get back · 10 min · 5 on 6

    The other half of Timnath: they push after every make or miss. Play 5 on 5 half court. On any make or miss, a coach outlets to the JV and they push, with a sixth player leaking out early.

    • Sprint back, stop the ball first, then find your man.
    • 1 point every time we stop the ball before half court.
